**About Me:** I was born in Rasht, Gilan, Iran. My childhood was spent disassembling my own toys and being curious about how they worked. I was also somehow addicted to sci-fi cartoons and movies. These things had a significant impact on my educational path and lifestyle. I started programming at the age of 13. Contributing to the field of robotics has always been one of my biggest dreams, as has living in a world where robots are everywhere. As a result, I pursued my high school education in STEM and continued this exciting path while earning my BSc in Mechanical Engineering and, currently, while completing my MSc in Mechatronics Engineering. I believe I have an important mission in this world, and I hope to be able to tackle the enormous obstacles, uncertainties, and dangers on my path.
